# App Store Optimization

Improve discoverability and conversion using the evidence actually available.

## Workflow

1. Identify the store, markets, app category, audience, positioning, and current listing.
2. Separate supplied or live evidence from modeled estimates. Never invent rankings, search volume, conversion, competitor performance, or review trends.
3. Diagnose the main constraint: relevance, discoverability, conversion, reputation, localization, or launch readiness.
4. Prioritize a small set of changes with the expected mechanism and a measurement plan.
5. Verify current store metadata rules and policy limits when exact compliance matters; do not rely on remembered character counts or historical rules.
6. For experiments, define one primary hypothesis, success metric, guardrails, duration logic, and stopping criteria.
7. For localization, adapt search intent and positioning rather than translating literally.

## Guardrails

- Label proxy metrics, estimates, and assumptions.
- Do not promise ranking or conversion outcomes.
- Keep keyword recommendations relevant to the product; avoid stuffing.
- Treat reviews as evidence, not proof of causality.
- When current store rules, pricing, or policies matter, verify them from current platform documentation.

## Output

Give the diagnosis, prioritized actions, revised metadata when requested, experiment or measurement plan, and important assumptions or data gaps.
